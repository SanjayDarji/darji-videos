## 1. Topic and unique angle
What the share market actually is, answered through one question beginners ask: "Is it gambling?" Angle: a share market is a *mandi* (marketplace) where small pieces of companies are traded. It is not a casino, but the risk is real, so learn first.

## 2. Target audience and objective
Hindi-speaking beginners (18–40) who hear about stocks but have never understood the basics. Objective: a clear mental model in under a minute, trust in the TheSDTrader brand, and saves and shares of a foundation video.

## 3. Three hook options
1. **"क्या शेयर बाज़ार जुआ है? पहले समझो, यह असल में है क्या।"** / "Is the stock market gambling? First understand what it actually is."
2. "सब्ज़ी मंडी और शेयर बाज़ार दोनों एक जैसे हैं, बस बिकता क्या है, यह अलग है।" / "A vegetable mandi and the stock market work alike. Only what is sold is different."
3. "9 जुलाई 1875 को शुरू हुआ भारत का सबसे पुराना शेयर बाज़ार। देखो यह कैसे काम करता है।" / "On 9 July 1875, India's oldest stock exchange began. See how it works."

## 4. Recommended hook
**Hook 1.** It is a direct question with emotional charge, a belief most beginners already hold, and it promises an honest answer. It is shown as large on-screen text with the face card in the first second.
