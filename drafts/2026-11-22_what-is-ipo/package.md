## 1. Topic and unique angle
What an IPO is, how you apply, and three things beginners get wrong: GMP is unofficial, a prospectus exists to be read, and applying does not mean you get shares.

## 2. Target audience and objective
Hindi-speaking beginners who hear about IPOs and "GMP" on the news and social media. Objective: a clear picture of what an IPO is and a healthy caution, with no hype. Saves and shares.

## 3. Three hook options
1. **"आईपीओ का नाम रोज़ सुनते हैं। पर आईपीओ असल में होता क्या है?"** / "You hear 'IPO' every day. But what is an IPO, really?"
2. "आईपीओ में आवेदन किया, पर शेयर नहीं मिले? यह क्यों होता है?" / "Applied for an IPO but got no shares? Why does this happen?"
3. "GMP देखकर आईपीओ भरना सही है?" / "Is it right to apply for an IPO by looking at GMP?"

## 4. Recommended hook
**Hook 1.** It is neutral and educational. Hook 3 is more clickable but leans towards a buy decision, so it was not used.
