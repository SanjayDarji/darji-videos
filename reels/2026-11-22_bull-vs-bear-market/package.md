## 11. Chart / data requirements and sources
No chart, no index level and no past market data. Facts used:
- Bear market is commonly a fall of 20% or more from a recent high: https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/smart-money/bear-vs-bull-market
- There is no formal metric; 20% is only sometimes used: https://www.dictionary.com/articles/bull-market-vs-bear-market
- Bull has no strict definition; some sources use a 20% rise from lows: https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/smart-money/bear-vs-bull-market and https://www.dictionary.com/e/bull-market-vs-bear-market/
- The 20% threshold is a convention, not a scientific boundary: https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en-gb/trading-terms/what-is-a-bear-market
The sources describe the S&P 500 and general usage; the video states the idea generally and does not claim an Indian-index rule.
