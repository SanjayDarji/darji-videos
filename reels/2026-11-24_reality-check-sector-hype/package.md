## Fact check (every number on screen)
- PSU: rally Apr 2020 to Jan 2024, 42% annualised (283% absolute); net withdrawal Rs 2,616 crore during the rally; net inflow Rs 5,356 crore Jan to late Sep 2024. Infrastructure: rally Apr 2020 to Jul 2023, 41% annualised (213% absolute); net withdrawal Rs 405 crore during the rally; net inflow Rs 16,041 crore Aug 2023 to Sep 2024; category fell about 24% by early March 2025 after the Sep 2024 correction. **Source: Value Research cover story "Sectoral funds: hot returns, cold reality" (fetched and read).** Note: the article's introduction quotes different since-June-2022 figures than its table (174% and 147% versus 56% and 46%); the video uses none of those, only the rally-period and flow figures from the body text.
- BSE Realty peak 13,647 in Jan 2008 and "about 4,000, an 11-year high but well below the peak" in Oct 2021: **ANAROCK (Anuj Puri), 20 Oct 2021** (search result text). March 2009 level 1,347: **GoodReturns** (search result text). The 90% fall is **my own calculation**: (13,647 - 1,347) / 13,647 = 90.1%.
- "Fund manager cannot switch sector", "sector funds are concentrated": scheme mandate; **Business Standard (Apr 2025)**, **Business Today (Mar 2026)**. "Many experts suggest a small share": **Business Today (12 Mar 2026), 5 to 10%**; **Business Standard (Dec 2023), up to 10 to 20%**. Shown as others' views and labelled not advice.
- Value Research's Sep 2026 note that the BSE Realty index "still hasn't recovered" came from a search snippet only (page fetch failed), so it is **not used** in the video or caption.
