## Fact check
- **Rule of 72:** years to double ≈ 72 / annual rate (%). It is an approximation, most accurate around 6-10% (Bankrate and others). Exact doubling time = ln 2 / ln(1+r): 6% → 11.9 yrs, 8% → 9.0, 12% → 6.1, 24% → 3.2 yrs.
- **3-year doubling:** 72 ÷ 3 = 24%; exact = 2^(1/3) − 1 = 26.0% a year.
- **Inflation:** MoSPI press release, CPI (base 2024) for October 2026 = 6.09% year on year (provisional), published 12 Nov 2026; first breach of RBI's 6% upper limit under the new series. 72 ÷ 6.09 = 11.8 yrs (exact 11.7). "Halving" is hypothetical, only if inflation stays at this level.
- **FD:** SBI 1-year FD (general public) 6.25% p.a., Business Today (22 Aug 2026) and other rate pages. Rates change; shown as "Aug 2026". 72 ÷ 6.25 = 11.5 yrs. 6.25 − 6.09 = 0.16 percentage points, before tax; real gain roughly zero only if both numbers stay as they are.
- **SEBI:** regulator's statement that there can be no guarantees of assured returns in the securities market (Business Standard); SEBI chairman's Oct 2026 warning on unrealistic return claims (Moneylife / FPJ).
