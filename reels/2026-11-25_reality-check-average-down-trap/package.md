## Fact check (every number on screen)
- **Recovery maths (own calculation):** needed gain = 1/(1 - fall) - 1: 20% to 25.0%, 40% to 66.7%, 50% to 100.0%, 80% to 400.0%. Matches the tables on Sahi (40% to 66.7%, 50% to 100%) and 5paisa (20% to 25%, 50% to 100%, 80% to 400%).
- **Example (own calculation, illustrative):** 100 shares at Rs 100 = Rs 10,000; price Rs 60; buy 100 more = Rs 16,000 for 200 shares, average cost Rs 80; break-even rise from Rs 60: 33.3% (versus 66.7% for the first lot alone). If the price falls to Rs 30: loss without averaging = 100 x (100 - 30) = Rs 7,000 (70% of Rs 10,000); loss with averaging = Rs 16,000 - Rs 6,000 = Rs 10,000 (62.5% of Rs 16,000). The caveat that averaging lowers the percentage loss but raises the rupee loss and doubles exposure is the same point Sahi makes.
- **Yes Bank:** all-time high Rs 404 on 20 Aug 2018: **Business Standard** (Sep 2018 market reports and company page). Price Rs 17.63 on 13 Nov 2026 (Upstox, 10:30 IST). Fall: (404 - 17.63) / 404 = 95.6%, shown as "about 96%". Historical price example only; no cause or recommendation stated. The all-time-low figure differs by source (Rs 5.55 vs Rs 5.65), so it is **not used**.
- **"Would you buy it again today at this price?" test:** widely used heuristic, also stated by Sahi; framed as a thinking framework, not advice.
