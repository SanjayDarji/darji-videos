## Fact check (every claim on screen)
- Regular plans carry distributor commission inside the expense ratio; direct plans do not; the cost comes out of daily NAV with no separate invoice: **Business Today (11 Aug 2026)**, **Value Research** (expense ratio deducted daily from NAV, charged regardless of performance), **AMFI** (TER is a percentage of daily net assets).
- Same portfolio, same fund manager, only the distributor differs: **The Ken (19 Nov 2026)**, HDFC Mutual Fund explainer (regular TER = direct TER plus distributor commission).
- Switch regular to direct = redemption plus fresh purchase, so capital gains tax may apply; exit load was removed on such switches in 2025: **Value Research**, **ClearTax**, **5paisa**.
- SIP example is **my own calculation**: Rs 10,000 per month, 240 months, month-end SIP, monthly compounding. 12% gives Rs 98.93 lakh, 11% gives Rs 86.56 lakh, gap Rs 12.36 lakh, invested Rs 24 lakh. It uses the same 12% vs 11% / 20-year assumption as Business Today (13 Nov 2026), but **I did not copy that article's table**: its figures (98.9 and 87.4, gap 11.5) mix two SIP-timing conventions and do not add up consistently. The video labels the example illustrative and gives no guarantee.
